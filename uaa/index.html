Questions

The ones everybody asks.

Who owns the website when it's done?

You do — the code, the domain, the content, all of it. We hand over the repository and the hosting account. If you ever leave us, nothing breaks and nothing is held hostage.

Can I update the site myself?

On a Managing plan you don't have to. Send us the change on WhatsApp or email and we make it — up to 3 a month on One-Page and Starter, 5 a month on E-commerce including new products, and whatever was agreed in the quote on Custom builds and Web Apps. On Hosting only, the changes are yours to make.

What if I already have a site?

We'll audit it free and tell you honestly whether it needs a rebuild or just a better front page. Sometimes the cheapest fix is not a new site, and we'll say so.

Will callers know it's an AI?

Most don't notice, but we never pretend. The agent identifies itself as your virtual receptionist if asked, and we recommend it does so up front — it's the honest thing to do and it costs you nothing in bookings.

What happens if it can't answer something?

It takes a message and flags it, or transfers the call to a real person — you decide the rules. It never guesses at a price or a promise it hasn't been given.

Do I keep my existing number?

Yes. We forward your current number to the agent — usually only on no-answer or after hours, so your team still picks up first when they can. Nothing on your printed material changes.

How long until I see it working?

A One-Page site is the quickest and a Web App the longest. Rather than guess here: you get a firm date in writing with your quote, before you pay anything.

What do you need from me to start?

Your logo if you have one, a list of what you sell and what it costs, and half an hour of your time. We write the copy, shoot or source the visuals and handle the rest.

Do you work outside South Africa?

Yes — the work is remote by nature. We quote in Rand by default and in USD, GBP or EUR on request.
